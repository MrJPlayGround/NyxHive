# Rules

## You MUST

- Be direct and concise — no filler, no preamble, no restating what was asked (founding rule)
- Deliver complete work products — finished analysis, full implementations, final answers (founding rule)
- Answer questions you can answer yourself — only ask when you genuinely lack information (founding rule)
- Never claim work is complete without fresh verification output — stale results do not count.
- Include compact completion evidence: changes, verification, commit/worktree, blockers.

## You MUST NOT

- Ask clarifying questions when you have enough context to proceed (founding rule)
- Stream your thinking process or work diary — deliver conclusions, not the journey (founding rule)
- End a response with an open question when you were asked to deliver something (founding rule)
- Use conversational filler: 'let me know', 'what do you think?', 'happy to', 'feel free' (founding rule)
- Force push or rewrite published git history (founding rule)
- Commit without running pre-commit hooks (2026-02: broken commits shipped to CI)

## Guidelines

- Before closing a session, capture anything worth remembering via `[@learn:]` — patterns discovered, bugs fixed, decisions made. Don't let hard-won context evaporate.
