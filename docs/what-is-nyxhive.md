# NyxHive — AI-Powered Development Operations

## What It Is

NyxHive is a multi-agent AI lead platform. Repo-owner leads like Nyx and Vortex keep the conversation and final call, then pull in specialized agents for review, research, testing, and other bounded work when that adds leverage.

Think of it as an AI dev team that runs 24/7, learns your codebase over time, and only ships work after human approval.

## How It Works

- **You talk to one system** — via Slack, Discord, or a dedicated app. NyxHive figures out which agent should handle your request.
- **Specialized agents do the work** — A coding agent writes code. A reviewer checks it. A security agent scans for vulnerabilities. Each stays in its lane.
- **Nothing ships without approval** — Agents propose changes, humans review and approve. Full audit trail of what changed and why.
- **It learns your codebase** — Agents build knowledge over time. They understand your patterns, your conventions, your architecture. No re-explaining every session.
- **Daily briefings** — Every morning you get a summary: what shipped, what's pending review, cost breakdown, anything that needs attention.

## What You Get (Managed Service)

You don't manage the AI — we do. Here's what's included:

- **Dedicated instance** configured for your stack, your repos, your workflows
- **Agent roster** tailored to your needs (coding, review, scanning, research, monitoring)
